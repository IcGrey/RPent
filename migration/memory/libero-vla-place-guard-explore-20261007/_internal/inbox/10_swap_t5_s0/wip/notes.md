## Attempt 1

Initial perception was reliable: agentview SAM grounded the upright black book at about `[-0.053, -0.013, 0.967]`, wrist refinement stayed within ~2.5 cm, and manual agentview topology picked the rear/upper cell of the split middle caddy column at about `[-0.463, -0.145, 0.998]`. The upright pick and high carry worked: `pi0_pick("pick up the book")` left a ~0.031 m gap, the book stayed visible in the wrist, and rim-clear vertical/high-midpoint moves preserved the grasp. The handoff failed after placement: two `pi0_place("place the book in the back compartment of the caddy")` windows of 20 steps each exhausted budget with no opening, and the VLA moved the EEF from the rear anchor toward `x≈-0.427, y≈-0.163` while the book began dividerward/frontward tilt. After a valid `scripted_fallback` declaration, release opened to ~0.078 m but did not fire; the straight-up open retreat revealed the book had fallen to the tabletop left/front of the caddy, not into the rear cell.

In-place recovery from the fallen flat book was noisy and should not be copied into a clean recipe. The dropped book segmented at about `[-0.264, -0.228, 0.955]`. A full-task-language re-pick closed nearly fully (`opening≈0.001 m`) while the book stayed on the tabletop, so it was declared `empty_gripper`. A generic retry then produced only a partial lift (`peak_lift≈0.034 m`); a firm-and-vertical retention test ended with the gripper fully shut and the book still on the table. Observation-bound wall: from this fallen-flat pose, the tried Pi0 pick prompts/poses were unreliable.

Next named lever: from a clean reset, keep the successful upright pick/carry, but stage the handoff farther rear in the split middle cell and use a more explicit rear/middle placement prompt so the VLA has less room to drift into the front paired cell before any guarded fallback.

After a valid `scripted_fallback` declaration, release opened to `≈0.078 m` but did not terminate, and the open vertical retreat left the book leaning outside/under the rear cell rather than inside it.

Observation-bound wall: under this method, continuing a second VLA window after the first no-open descent lets Pi0 drift too far negative-y; fallback release from `y≈-0.20` misses. Next named lever: from a clean reset, keep the successful upright pick/carry and rear handoff, but after the first 20-step VLA budget exhaustion either (a) skip the second drift window, or (b) while still holding, re-center closed at the agentview rear-cell anchor (`x≈-0.469,y≈-0.148`) before declaring scripted fallback and releasing. Do not copy the release from the drifted `y≈-0.20` pose.
