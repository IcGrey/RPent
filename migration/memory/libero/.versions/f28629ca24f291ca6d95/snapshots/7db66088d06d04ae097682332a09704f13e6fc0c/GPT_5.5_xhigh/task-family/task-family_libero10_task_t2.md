## Applicable pattern

Activate a stove knob with learned contact control, grasp a frying-pan handle, and center a large asymmetric payload on a cook region despite yaw-dependent reach walls and changing held offsets.

## Winning technique

Semantically identify the black frying pan, spiral cook region, black knob, and moka distractor in agentview; refine handle and burner geometry with wrist points accepted only within 5 cm. Activate first from home with a repurposed contact pick (12 chunks plus single-chunk continuations until the coil is visibly red). Pre-position about 6-10 cm above the handle root, grasp with the short prompt, verify an object-sized finger gap and handle in the wrist image, firm for 8-12 steps, and lift vertically. Rotate +90 degrees twice, then if direct centering hits the yawed reach wall rotate back -90 degrees. At that reachable yaw, remeasure pan-body minus EEF offset, servo to burner-minus-offset, and remeasure again after each loaded correction. Apply residual corrections while airborne. Descend vertically in short support probes with +1 held; release only when the level pan stops descending over the coil.

## Magic numbers

- Stove contact: max_chunks=12 (usable band 10-14), then max_chunks=1 continuations (band 1-3) until RGB shows a red coil.
- Grasp-only call: max_chunks=8 (usable band 8-12), lift_thresh=0.05, gripper_closed_thresh=0.06.
- Handle prepose: eef z about 1.00 in this kitchen frame (usable band 0.99-1.03); re-derive xy every scene.
- Firm grip: steps=10 (usable band 8-12).
- Loaded moves: step_clip=0.003-0.006; yaw step_clip=0.08 (usable band 0.06-0.10).
- Accept wrist refinement only within 0.03-0.05 m of its agentview identity anchor.
- Recompute body offset after every large yaw or loaded servo; residual corrections of 0.02-0.06 m were needed.
- NEVER carry with gripper=-1.
- NEVER descend after assuming a rigid offset survived a loaded servo; remeasure first.
- NEVER judge stove activation from knob contact alone; require the coil to be visibly red.
